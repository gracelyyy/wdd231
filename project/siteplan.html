Laid with Grace Site Plan

Site Name

Laid with Grace

Laid with Grace is a beauty and hairstyling brand focused on helping clients feel confident and beautiful through professional hair services and beauty care. The name reflects elegance, creativity, and self-expression.

Site Purpose

The website will provide information about hairstyling and beauty services, showcase a portfolio of completed hairstyles, allow clients to book appointments, display pricing information, and provide contact details.

Scenarios

  • What hairstyling services does Laid with Grace offer, and what are the prices?
  • How can I book an appointment for a hairstyle or beauty service?
  • Can I view examples of previous hairstyles before choosing a service?
  • What hair care products and maintenance tips are recommended after my appointment?
